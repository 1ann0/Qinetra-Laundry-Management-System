## Important design constraints and gaps

1. **Free uptime has a budget.** [Render](https://render.com/docs/free) spins idle services down after 15 minutes and grants 750 shared hours/month. Two continuously awake APIs would need roughly 1,440–1,488 hours. Do not install an unlimited keep-alive or wake demo/practice services routinely. The older human usage reading (6.27 hours) is not a current balance. A read-only health probe may wake a server; it must be bounded too.
2. **Photos need independent recovery.** Current `ops.backup.export_database` uses `pg_dump --schema=qlms`; it does not archive Storage objects. Store photos outside Render's ephemeral filesystem. Proposed initial limits: optional, at most **3 images/order, 300 KB/image**, maximum long edge **1280 px**, re-encode and strip metadata, private Supabase bucket through the existing backend authority. Validate bytes/dimensions server-side as well as compressing in the browser. The app's custom JWT is not Supabase Auth; don't assume generic auth.uid storage policies grant the right access.
3. **Proposed photo retention needs approval before implementation:** **90 days after collection**, unresolved holds/disputes excluded until resolved. At 20 orders/week for 13 weeks, 3 × 300 KB gives roughly **234 MB** before exceptions/overhead; not an actual usage forecast. [Supabase Free includes 1 GB file storage](https://supabase.com/pricing). Copying a full 234 MB bucket into each of 90 daily backups would exceed the 5 GB Drive allowance. Qualify encrypted object archives stored once with retained manifests/reference-aware cleanup, or another measured bounded strategy, before release. DB-only recovery cannot count as photo recovery.
4. **Close is a record, not payroll/inventory/accounting automation.** Bulk soap is an expense when paid; sachets and transport are separate actual purchases. Wages are actual payments with period/person notes. Till withdrawals are separate from expenses. Existing receipts must not be relabelled profit or historical opening balances invented. M-Pesa statement totals require Owner entry and comparison.
5. **Public tracking and photos change the privacy boundary.** Do not put photos or private staff data on the link. Keep it small; the previous no-customer-portal rule is amended only for the explicitly requested read-only status view.
6. **Only targeted refactoring.** Keep current components/helpers. Extract shared money/closing calculations where required so dashboard/report/export agree; avoid a broad main.py or frontend rewrite. Keep catalogue, service units, order-level payment/refund allocation and correction history semantics.

For each increment, record its short design and exact touched callers/tests/config, write a failing regression/fixture where practical, implement the smallest complete change, run focused checks plus affected regressions, review money/permission/concurrency boundaries, rehearse compatible migration and rollback, deploy exact CI-verified source, and verify the actual hosted frontend/API/schema combination. Review at 375px plus the work phone for hardware-dependent camera/download behavior. Preserve inputs and useful next actions, 44px touch targets, clear labels, appropriate keyboards, accessible errors/status and reduced-motion support. Do not treat a phone-sized desktop viewport as actual Android evidence.

For money, use a hand-calculated mixed-service/partial-payment/refund/date-boundary fixture. For permissions, test HTTP denial as well as hidden navigation. For saves, simulate a response lost after commit and verify one stored result after retry. No fake production customers, costs or payments. Rollback normally restores the previous compatible API/frontend while retaining added tables and their history, not dropping data.
