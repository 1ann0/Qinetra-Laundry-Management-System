# Owner and Employee priorities

14 October 2026. Source-based review for the approved pilot plan, not a new architecture or feature expansion.

The existing workflow covers intake, mixed services, clothing condition/custody, ready promises, holds, outsourcing, receipts, partial cash/M-Pesa payments, debts, corrections/refunds, customer history and role restrictions. Shared Find already searches customers, phone, tickets and services. Owner-only catalogue and Staff controls exist. Read the release records to distinguish deployed features from local work.

The dashboard/report increment closes the immediate Owner visibility gap: today's work, production delays, uncollected laundry, customer questions, outsourcing issues, current money owed both ways and dated receipts/returns with downloads. It does not infer profit or verify M-Pesa transactions. Employees retain a short Home with intake, tracking and customer lookup; Owner figures and configuration remain restricted.

The essential remaining money controls are already approved: expense corrections, till additions/withdrawals, counted-cash daily closing and Owner statement-total comparison. They should expose a shortage, keep the original closing snapshot, and flag later corrections for review. These matter before membership margin guesses or extra decorative charts.

The isolated hosted phone demo, download/open acceptance on Android and subsequent membership trial also remain in the agreed queue. Demo privacy, reset destinations and free hosting budget must be proved before external presentations. Membership money and order payments must be counted once, with atomic allowance deduction and preserved paid terms.

The new receipt request should reuse `frontend/src/lib/order-receipt.js` and both existing sharing callers. Use a consistent short heading, ticket/customer, saved service quantities/charges, clothing/condition details, total/paid/balance/refund facts, Nairobi ready promise/status, and Owner-configured public contact, collection directions and opening hours. Optional Owner greeting/footer must not replace saved financial facts. Preview and recipient check come before manually opening WhatsApp; QLMS must not claim the message was delivered. No automatic customer contact, invented hours/contact/payment instructions, public ticket link or internal staff notes.

After the approved features are deployed, revisit optional preferences (customer cleaning instructions), supplies stock, task responsibility and pickup/delivery only with a concrete shop need. Do not implement those now. A mobile usability review should continue feature by feature: visible search, readable empty states, clear next action, generous controls and preserved uncertain-save references.
